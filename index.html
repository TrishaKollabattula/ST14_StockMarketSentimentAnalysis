About Stock Market Sentiment

Stock market sentiment refers to the overall attitude of investors toward a particular security or financial market. It is the feeling or tone of a market, or its crowd psychology, as revealed through the activity and price movement of the securities traded in that market.

Understanding market sentiment is crucial for making informed investment decisions. Our sentiment analysis tool helps you predict market trends based on the latest data and news.
