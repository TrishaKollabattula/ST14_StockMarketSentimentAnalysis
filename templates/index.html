Stock Market Sentiment Analysis

Analyze the sentiment of stock market news and determine market trends.

Stock market sentiment analysis uses natural language processing (NLP) to understand the mood behind financial news, stock tweets, and market-related reports. This can be helpful for traders and investors to make informed decisions about their investments based on public sentiment.

By analyzing social media, news articles, and other sources of data, we can predict the overall market mood, whether it's positive, negative, or neutral. This prediction can help investors gauge market movements before they happen.
